> Two events $e_1$ and $e_2$ are **concurrent** (written as $e_1 || e_2$) iff:
> $$ ~(e_1 -> e_2) and ~(e_2 -> e_1) $$

Just like in [[8. Special Relativity|special relativity]], how two events that occur in a close spacetime interval $Delta s < 0$ cannot possibly influence each other, two programmatic events that occur with a close "computational interval" cannot be [[Causality|causally]] linked.
