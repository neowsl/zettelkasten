[[Emacs and Vim in the Age of AI]] talks about the role that editors like [[Vim]] and [[Emacs]] plays in an LLM-driven coding era. I resonate with the author's points; some significant part of my interest in programming is driven by the ability to [[customise]] my tools however I want.

Furthermore, what differentiates an "experienced" software engineer from a junior? Perhaps learning the tools is no longer the differentiator, and now there is a heavier emphasis on field expertise and system design.
