## Formation of the Earth

1. Space dust collides due to gravity, increasing internal temperature
2. Internal temperature rises enough to melt metals
3. Denser metals fall towards centre of Earth

We have never drilled to the centre of the Earth, but we have evidence for what we believe would be at the centre.

Earth has a density of $5.5 "g"/"cm"^3$.
- The Earth's crust has a density between 2.6 - 3.0
- The uppermost mantle is between 3.0 - 3.3
Therefore, the centre of the Earth must be made of a denser material than the outer layers.

We collect samples from meteorites to understand what materials were available to make up the Earth.

Magnetic field of Earth indicates convecting magnetic metals in Earth's core.

## Earthquakes

**P-waves:** Go *through* the Earth, think push-pull.
**S-waves:** Motion perpendicular to the direction of the wave.

The lithosphere (crust) is in isostatic equilibrium with the asthenosphere (mantle). Like a ferry that sinks when cars drive onto it, the crust "sinks" when there is more sediment deposited onto it.

The lithosphere grows in the oceans, but the Earth isn't growing, so the excess material gets buried into **subduction zones**.
