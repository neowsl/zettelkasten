We can compare minerals to cells in the human body. Cells (organic chemistry) are mostly built from carbon, oxygen, and hydrogen. On the other hand, minerals are built primarily from silicon and oxygen.

Two main ways to classify minerals:
1. Chemical composition
2. Atomic arrangement

Three secondary ways:
1. Must be solid with a crystal/lattice structure
2. Must be inorganic - e.g. clam shells don't count
3. Naturally occurring - e.g. minerals made in labs don't count

Two ways of formation:
1. Crystallisation from magma melt
    - **Inclusion:** Inside crystal must be older than outside crystal
2. Precipitation out of an aqueous solution

**Only 7** crystal forms that define basically all the minerals of the world.
