Andesite forms from fast-cooling divergent ocean plates, while granite forms from continental plate collision. Mountains like the Himalayas don't erupt because the granite solidifies well before it reaches the surface.

**Bowen's reaction series:** This is a stacked area graph that displays the composition of minerals. Notice you can infer some mineral properties from this diagram - e.g. quartz and olivine will not appear in the same mineral.

Tectonic plates can change directions if an entire plate gets consumed, e.g. Hawaiian islands.

## Collapse of a volcano

Volcanoes accumulate more and more silica content until there's such a large explosion that it ejects the magma base, resulting in a volcanic collapse. This can be seen at Mount Mazama, which is now Crater Lake.

**Basalt columns** form due to cooling and contraction of basaltic lava flows, which form polygonal columns to relieve tension most efficiently.

**Pillow basalts** form when lava cools underwater; the outer shell crystallises quickly, but the inner lava remains ductile and pushes the shell into pillow/bubble shapes.
