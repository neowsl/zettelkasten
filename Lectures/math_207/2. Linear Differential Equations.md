## 2026-01-12

> A **linear differential equation** of order 1 can be written in the form
> $$ y' + p(x)y = g(x). $$

We can use the **product rule**, which says that $(f g)' = f'g + g'f$.

1. Find the integrating factor:

$$ mu(x) = e^(integral p(x) d x) $$

2. Multiply by the integrating factor
3. Integrate both sides
4. Divide by the integrating factor
