> The **convolution** ($*$) of two functions $f$ and $g$ is defined as
> $$ (f * g)(t) = integral_0^t f(t - tau) g(tau) d tau $$

This is useful because although you can't directly multiply Laplace transforms, you can use the following relationship:
$$ laplace(f * g) = laplace(f) dot laplace(g) $$
