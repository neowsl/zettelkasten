## 2026-05-11: Regular Expressions

Subsets of strings are called **languages**.

**Regular expressions** over $Sigma$:
- Basis:
	- $epsilon$ is a regular expression (could also include $emptyset$).
	- $a$ is a regular expression for any $a in Sigma$.
- Recursive step:
	- If $A$ and $B$ are regular expressions, then so are:
		- $A union B$ (matches all strings that match either $A$ or $B$ or both)
		- $A B$ (matches all strings where the first part is $A$ and the second part is $B$)
		- $A^*$ (matches all strings that have any number of adjacent $A$s)

## 2026-05-15: Relations and Graphs

> A **binary relation** between $A$ and $B$ is a subset of $A times B$.

Let $R$ be a relation on $A$.
- $R$ is **reflexive** iff $(a, a) in R$ for every $a in A$.
- $R$ is **symmetric** iff $(a, b) in R$ implies $(b, a) in R$.
- $R$ is **antisymmetric** iff ${} (a, b) in R {}$ and $a != b$ implies $(b, a) in.not R$
- $R$ is **transitive** iff $(a, b) in R$ and $(b, c) in R$ implies $(a, c) in R$.

Let $R$ be a relation from $A$ to $B$.
Let $S$ be a relation from $B$ to $C$.
The **composition** of $R$ and $S$, $R compose S$ is the relation from $A$ to $C$ defined by:

$$ R compose S = {(a, c) : exists b "such that" (a, b) in R "and" (b, c) in S} $$

$R^2 := R compose R$
$R^0 := {(a, a) : a in A}$
$R^(n + 1) := {R^n compose R} "for" n >= 0$

### Functions

A **function** $f : A -> B$ is a special type of relation from $A$ to $B$.
For every $a in A$, there must be *exactly one* $b in B$ with $(a, b) in f$.

### Directed graphs

$$ G = (V, E) $$
Where:
- $V$ is the vertices
- ${} E$ is the edges

A **path** is $v_0, v_1, ..., v_k$ with each $(v_i, v_(i + 1))$ in $E$.
**Simple path**: No repeated $v$
**Cycle**: $v_0 = v_k$
**Simple cycle**: A cycle with no repeated $v$ other than the last one

$R^n$ is paths of length $n$.

The **length** of a path is the number of edges in it.

The **connectivity relation** $R^*$ consists of the pairs $(a, b)$ such that $b$ is reachable from $a$ in $R$.

$$ R^* = union_(k = 0)^infinity R^k $$

The **transitive-reflexive closure** of a relation $R$ is the connectivity relation $R^*$.

## 2026-05-18: Finite State Machines

Also called DFAs: Deterministic Finite Automata

Must have:
- States
- Transitions on input signals
- Start state and final states

Each machine is designed for strings over some fixed alphabet ${} Sigma$.
- Must have a transition from each state for *every* symbol in $Sigma$.

NFA (Nondeterministic Finite Automata) aren't required to have exactly 1 edge out of each state labelled by each symbol in $Sigma$.
