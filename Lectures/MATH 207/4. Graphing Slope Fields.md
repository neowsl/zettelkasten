> A first-order DE in the form
> $$ (d y) / (d t) = f(y) $$
> is called **autonomous**: the independent variable $t$ does not appear explicitly.

> The constant solutions to a DE are called **equilibrium solutions**. They correspond to the zeroes of $f$.

If $y(t) = c$, then $y'(t) = 0$ is a solution to the DE.

If $y'(t) != 0$,

A solution is **increasing** when $y'(t) > 0$.
A solution is **decreasing** when $y'(t) < 0$.

Note: when finding the second derivative $y''(t)$, we can't derive wrt $y$, we must treat $y$ as a function of $t$, then derive wrt $t$.
