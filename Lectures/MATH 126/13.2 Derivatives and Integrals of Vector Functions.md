Unit tangent vector $arrow(T)(t) = (arrow(r)'(t)) / (|arrow(r)'(t)|)$

> **Derivative of a vector function**
> Let $arrow(r)(t) = angle.l f(t), g(t), h(t) angle.r$ be a vector function. The **derivative** $arrow(r)'(t)$ is:
> $$ arrow(r)'(t) = angle.l f'(t), g'(t), h'(t) angle.r $$

> **Integral of a vector function**
> The **integral** $integral arrow(r)(t) d t$ is:
> $$ integral arrow(r)(t) d t = angle.l integral f(t) d t, integral g(t) d t, integral h(t) d t angle.r $$

Remember:
$$ f'(x) = lim_(h arrow 0) (f(x + h) - f(x)) / h $$

Similarly,
$$ arrow(r)'(t) = lim_(h arrow 0) (arrow(r)(t + h) - f(t)) / h $$

$arrow(r)'(t)$ = the direction of the tangent line = the tangent vector

## Position, velocity, and acceleration

Position: $arrow(r)(t)$
Velocity: $arrow(v)(t) = arrow(r)'(t)$
Acceleration: $arrow(a)(t) = arrow(v)'(t) = arrow(r)''(t)$
