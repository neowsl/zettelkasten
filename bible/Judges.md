## 19 - 21

> [!bible] [Judges 21:25 - ESV](https://bolls.life/ESV/7/21/)
> 25. In those days there was no king in Israel. Everyone did what was right in his own eyes.

Terrible events lead to rape and murder of concubine by Benjaminite gang; tribes of Israel turn against tribe of Benjamin, civil war ensues.
Shows the evil in Israel without God; everyone acting according to their own morals leads to great evil.
