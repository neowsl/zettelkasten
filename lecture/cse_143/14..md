## grep

```sh
grep word
grep . # wildcard
grep -vi a # excludes a
grep -v ..a.. # words that don't have an a in the middle
```

## Regular expressions

```java
str.split(" +"); // splits by any number of spaces
str.split("\t"); // splits by tabs
str.split("[ \t]+"); // splits by spaces and tabs
str.split("[a-zA-Z]+"); // splits by letters
str.split("[^a-zA-Z]+"); // splits by NOT letters

Scanner scanner = new Scanner(System.in);
scanner.useDelimiter(","); // read by commas
```

## BNF grammars

Terminals and non-terminals

- Terminals = files = base case
- Non-terminals = directories = recursive case

### Linguistics

Sentence: `<s>::= <np> <vp>`

Noun phrase: `<np>::= <pn> | <article> <n> | <article> <adjp> <n>`

Proper noun: `<pn>::= Stuart Reges | Columbus | Napoleon | Hannibal Barca | The Earth | The University of Washington`

Noun: `<n>::= rock | ice cream | cat | monkey | baseball | jeans`

Article: `<article>::= the | a | some`

Verb phrase: `<vp>::= <tv> <np> | <iv> | <adv> <vp>`

Transitive verb: `<tv>::= bought | ate | sold | jumped over | wore`

Intransitive verb: `<iv>::= slept | laughed | ate | read | studied`

Adjective: `<adj>::= great | awesome | mediocre | bad | sad | lackluster | crunchy`

Adjective Phrase: `<adjp>::= <adj> | <adj> <adjp>`

Adverb: `<adv>::= quickly | brilliantly | sluggishly | slowly | painfully | weirdly`
