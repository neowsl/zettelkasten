## 2026-04-08

### Arithmetic overflow

Values to remember:
- **UMin** (min unsigned int) = 0
- **UMax** (max unsigned int) = $2^w - 1$
- **TMin** (max unsigned int) = $-2^(w - 1)$
- **TMax** (max unsigned int) = $2^(w - 1) - 1$

### Casting

Longer to shorter:
- Truncation (drops upper bytes)

Shorter to longer:
- Unsigned just pads with zeroes
- Signed pads with MSB

Interpretation change:
- Bits do not change!

---

- Note: Unsigned values "dominate" signed values when evaluating expressions.

### Bit shifting

Left shift: Fills in zeroes on the right.

Right shift:
- Logical shift (unsigned values): Fills in zeroes on the left.
- Arithmetic shift (signed values): Replicates the MSB on the left.
