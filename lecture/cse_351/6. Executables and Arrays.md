**CALL**: Compiler -> Assembler -> Linker -> Loader

Object files have:
- Symbol table: Holds list of "items" that may be used by other files
- Relocation table: Holds list of "items" that this file needs the address of

Given the following disassembly:
```
0000000000401126 <main>:
  401126:   48 83 ec 08         sub    $0x8,%rsp
  40112a:   bf 10 20 40 00      mov    $0x402010,%edi
  40112f:   e8 fc fe ff ff      callq  401030 <puts@plt>
  401134:   b8 00 00 00 00      mov    $0x0,%eax
  401139:   48 83 c4 08         add    $0x8,%rsp
  40113d:   c3                  retq
  40113e:   66 90               xchg   %ax,%ax
```

- The byte of data at address `0x40113d` is `c4`
- The immediate `0x402010` can be found at address `0x40112b`

Disassembly output is in little-endian. I.e. the byte at address `0x40112f` is `0xff`.
