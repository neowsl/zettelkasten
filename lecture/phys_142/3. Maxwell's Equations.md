## 2026-01-26

In a conductor, the net E-field everywhere is 0 (must be true for electrostatic equilibrium). This also means that the enclosed charge is 0, by Gauss's Law.

A hollow sphere behaves like a point charge outside its surface, but has 0 charge inside.

For an insulating sphere, $E = (rho r) / (3 epsilon_0)$, where $rho$ is a uniform volume charge density.
