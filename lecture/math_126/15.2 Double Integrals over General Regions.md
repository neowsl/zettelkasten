## Types of regions

### Type 1 regions

Bounded left and right by constants, top and bottom are functions.

$$ integral.double_D f(x, y) d A = integral_a^b integral_h(x)^g(x) f(x, y) d y d x $$

Where $h(x)$ is the bottom function and $g(x)$ is the top function.

### Type 2 regions

Bounded top and bottom by constants, left and right are functions.

$$ integral.double_D f(x, y) d A = integral_c^d integral_h(y)^g(y) f(x, y) d x d y $$

Where $h(y)$ is the left function and $g(y)$ is the right function.

![[15.2 Double Integrals over General Regions 2025-11-05 12.49.58.excalidraw]]

---

$$ integral_a^b integral_h(x)^g(x) f(x, y) d y d x $$

"For every $x$ between $a$ and $b$, $y$ ranges from $y = h(x)$ to $y = g(x)$."

$f(x, y)$ stays the same!

Note: The outer bounds must be constants. The inner integral's bounds should be in terms of the outer variable.
