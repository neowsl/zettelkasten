**Lamina**: A flat 2D region

> Let $D$ be a lamina in the $x y$-plane with density function $rho(x, y)$. To find the mass $m$ of $D$:
> $$ m = integral.double_D rho(x, y) d A $$

**Moment**: Mass times distance

$M_y$ = Total moment about $y$-axis = Tendency to rotate about the $y$-axis = Mass times distance to $y$-axis
$$ M_y = integral.double_D rho(x, y) x d A $$

$M_x$ = Total moment about $x$-axis = Tendency to rotate about the $x$-axis = Mass times distance to $x$-axis
$$ M_x = integral.double_D rho(x, y) y d A $$

> To find the centre of mass $(overline(x), overline(y))$ of $D$:
> $$ (overline(x), overline(y)) = (M_y / m, M_x / m) $$

Note: Many times, proportionality constants will cancel at the end.
