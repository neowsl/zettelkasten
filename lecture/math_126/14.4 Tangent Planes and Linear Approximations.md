> An equation of the **tangent plane** to a surface $z = f(x, y)$ at the point $(x_0, y_0)$ is:
> $$ f_x (x_0, y_0)(x - x_0) + f_y (x_0, y_0)(y - y_0) - (z - z_0) = 0 $$
> $$ z = f_x (x_0, y_0)(x - x_0) + f_y (x_0, y_0)(y - y_0) + z_0 $$

Normal vector to the tangent plane:

$$ angle.l f_x, f_y, -1 angle.r $$

## Linearisation

> The **linearisation** $L(x, y)$ of a function $f(x, y)$ at a point $(x_0, y_0)$ is given by:
> $$ L(x, y) = f_x (x_0, y_0)(x - x_0) + f_y (x_0, y_0)(y - y_0) + f(x_0, y_0) $$

Make sure to plug discovered points back into the equation to obtain the $z$-value!
