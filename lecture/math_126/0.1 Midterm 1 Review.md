## Distances

### Distance between point and point

$$ d(P, Q) = |arrow(P Q)| $$

### Distance between point and plane

$$ d(P, Sigma) = (|arrow(P Q)| dot arrow(n)) / (|arrow(n)|) $$

Where:
- $P$ is the singular point
- $Q$ is a point on the plane
- $arrow(n)$ is the normal vector of the plane

$$ d = (|A x_0 + B y_0 + C z_0 - D|) / sqrt(A^2 + B^2 + C^2) $$

Where:
- $A x + B y + C z = D$ is the equation of the plane
- $(x_0, y_0, z_0)$ is the singular point

### Distance between point and line

$$ d(P, L) = (|arrow(P Q) times arrow(u)|) / (|arrow(u)|) $$

Where:
- $P$ is the singular point
- $Q$ is a point on the line
- $arrow(u)$ is the direction vector of the line

### Point between line and line

$$ d(L, M) = (|arrow(P Q) dot (arrow(u) times arrow(v))) / (|arrow(u) times arrow(v)|) $$
