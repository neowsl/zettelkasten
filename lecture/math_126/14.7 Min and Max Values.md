## Critical Points

> The **critical points** $(x, y)$ of a function $f(x, y)$ are the points such that $f_x = 0$ and $f_y = 0$ *or* either $f_x$ or $f_y$ is undefined.

**Saddle point**: A point that is the maximum along one axis but the minimum along another axis. Looks like a saddle.

## Second Derivative Test

> The **Second Derivative Test** is used to find the local extrema of a function $f(x, y)$ by classifying the critical points in the following way:
> $$ D(a, b) - f_(x x) (a, b) dot f_(y y) (a, b) - (f_(x y) (a, b))^2 $$
> There are several cases:
> - $D(a, b) > 0$:
> 	- $f_(x x) (a, b) > 0$ -> local minimum
> 	- $f_(x x) (a, b) < 0$ -> local maximum
> - $D(a, b) < 0$ -> saddle point
> - $D(a, b) = 0$ -> no information (rarely occurs)
