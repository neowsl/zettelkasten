Three points $A$, $B$, and $C$ in $bb(R)^n$ lie in a straight line if and only if $|A B| + |B C| = |A C|$ (given $|A B|, |B C| < |A C|$).

## Angles

> **Dot product**
> The **dot product** between two vectors $arrow(u) = angle.l u_1, u_2, u_3 angle.r$ and $arrow(v) = angle.l v_1, v_2, v_3 angle.r$ is:
> $$ arrow(u) dot arrow(v) = u_1 v_1 + u_2 v_2 + u_3 v_3 $$
> Or also:
> $$ arrow(u) dot arrow(v) = |arrow(u)| |arrow(v)| cos(theta) $$
> I.e.:
>  $$ u_1 v_1 + u_2 v_2 + u_3 v_3 = |arrow(u)| |arrow(v)| cos(theta) $$

To solve for an angle:
$$ theta = arccos((arrow(u) dot arrow(v)) / (|arrow(u)| |arrow(v)|)) $$

$arrow(u) dot arrow(v)$ will be:
- **Positive** if both vectors point in the same direction
- **Zero** if the vectors are perpendicular
- **Negative** if the vectors point in opposite directions

**Magic angle**: ~0.95 rad (angle between edge and diagonal of a cube)

## Scalar component

> **Scalar component**
> The **scalar component** of vector $arrow(b)$ onto vector $arrow(a)$ is:
> $$ "comp"_arrow(a) arrow(b) = |arrow(b)| cos(theta) = (arrow(a) dot arrow(b)) / (|arrow(a)|) $$

> **Projection**
> The **projection** of vector $arrow(b)$ onto vector $arrow(a)$ is:
> $$ "proj"_arrow(a) arrow(b) = (arrow(a) dot arrow(b)) / (|arrow(a)|) times (arrow(a)) / (|arrow(a)|) = ((arrow(a) dot arrow(b)) / (|arrow(a)|^2)) arrow(a) = ((arrow(a) dot arrow(b)) / (arrow(a) dot arrow(a))) arrow(a) $$

- Note: $|arrow(a)|^2 = a_1^2 + a_2^2 + a_3^2 = arrow(a) dot arrow(a)$

## Orthagonality

> **Cross product**
> If $arrow(a) = angle.l a_1, a_2, a_3 angle.r$ and $arrow(b) = angle.l b_1, b_2, b_3 angle.r$, then the **cross product** $arrow(a) times arrow(b)$ is
> $$ mat(delim: "|", arrow(i), arrow(j), arrow(k); a_1, a_2, a_3; b_1, b_2, b_3) $$
> $$ = arrow(i) mat(delim: "|", a_2, a_3; b_2, b_3) - arrow(j) mat(delim: "|", a_1, a_3; b_1, b_3) + arrow(k) mat(delim: "|", a_1, a_2; b_1, b_2) $$
> $$ = arrow(i) (a_2 b_3 - a_3 b_2) - arrow(j) (a_1 b_3 - a_3 b_1) + arrow(k) (a_1 b_2 - a_2 b_1) $$
> $$ = angle.l a_2 b_3 - a_3 b_2, a_3 b_1 - a_1 b_3, a_1 b_2 - a_2 b_1 angle.r $$

- Note: $arrow(a) times arrow(b) = -(arrow(b) times arrow(a))$.
- Note: $|arrow(a) times arrow(b)| = |arrow(a)| |arrow(b)| sin theta$.
	- This is very similar to the [[12.1 Three-Dimensional Coordinate Systems#Angles|dot product]], except the $sin$ describes the angle to orthogonal vs. the angle to parallel.
- Note: You can check if you calculated the cross product correctly by calculating the dot product to both of the other vectors. Both dot products should be 0.

## Area of parallelogram

> To find the area of the parallelogram with vertices at points $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$:
> Let $arrow(a)$ be the vector from $A$ to $B$.
> Let $arrow(b)$ be the vector from $A$ to $D$.
> $$ "Area" = "base" dot "height" $$
> $$ = |arrow(a)| (|arrow(b)| sin theta) $$
> $$ = |arrow(a) times arrow(b)| $$
