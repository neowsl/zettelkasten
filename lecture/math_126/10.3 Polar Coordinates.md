The four main equations for converting a coordinate point between Cartesian coordinates and polar coordinates are:
1. $x = r cos theta$
2. $y = r sin theta$
3. $r = sqrt(x^2 + y^2)$
4. $tan theta = y / x$

Note: For negative $r$, add $pi$ to the angle.

> *Example*
> Convert the equation for the line to polar form. Write your final answer in the form $r$ as a function of $theta$.
> $$ y = 1/2 x - 1 $$

$r sin theta = 1/2 r cos theta - 1$
$r sin theta - 1/2 r cos theta = -1$
$r (sin theta - 1/2 cos theta) = -1$
$r = -1 / (sin theta - 1/2 cos theta)$

Note: It may be useful to create a graph of $r$ versus $theta$. It makes it easier to create the table of $r$ versus $theta$.

$r = cos(N theta)$ produces $N$ petals if $N$ is odd, and $2N$ petals if $N$ is even!
