## Hyperboloid

$$ x^2 / a^2 + y^2 / b^2 - z^2 / c^2 = 1 $$

## Elliptic paraboloid

$$ z / c = x^2 / a^2 + y^2 / b^2 $$

## Hyperbolic paraboloid

$$ z / c = x^2 / a^2 - y^2 / b^2 $$

## Elliptic cone

$$ z^2 / c = x^2 / a^2 + y^2 / b^2 $$

Trace: The 2D graph produced by setting one of the variables to a constant. I.e. the intersection of the surface and a plane. Useful for imagining how a 3D surface will look like.
