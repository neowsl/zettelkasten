> The **$n$-th Taylor polynomial** $T_n (x)$ of $f(x)$ at $x = b$ is:
> $$ T_n (x) = sum_(k = 0)^n (f^((k))(b)) / (k!) (x - b)^k $$

## Taylor's Inequality

> The upper bound on the error $|T_n (x) - f(x)$ on an interval containing $b$ is:
> $$ |T_1 (x) - f(x)| <= M / 2! |x - b|^2 "where" |f''(t)| <= M $$
> $$ |T_2 (x) - f(x)| <= M / 3! |x - b|^3 "where" |f'''(t)| <= M $$
> $$ |T_n (x) - f(x)| <= M / (n + 1)! |x - b|^(n + 1) "where" |f^((n + 1))(t) <= M $$
