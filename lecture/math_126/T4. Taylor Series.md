> The **Taylor series** of $f(x)$ based at $x = b$ is:
> $$ lim_(n arrow infinity) sum_(k = 0)^n f^((k)) / k! (x - b)^k $$

3 scenarios for convergence:
1. Converges for all $x$
2. Converges at $x = b$
3. Converges on interval centred at $x = b$

The **four main Taylor series** are:

| $y = f(x)$    | Taylor series $b = 0$                                    | Converges?  |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| $e^x$         | $$ sum_(k = 0)^infinity x^k / k! $$                      | All $x$     |
| $sin x$       | $$ sum_(k = 0)^infinity (-1)^k / (2k + 1)! x^(2k + 1) $$ | All $x$     |
| $cos x$       | $$ sum_(k = 0)^infinity (-1)^k / (2k)! x^(2k) $$         | All $x$     |
| $1 / (1 - x)$ | $$ sum_(k = 0)^infinity x^k $$                           | $\|x\| < 1$ |
