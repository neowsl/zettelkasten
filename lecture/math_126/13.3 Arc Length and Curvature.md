Many different ways of paramaterising a function. E.g. $arrow(r)(t) = angle.l cos t, sin t angle.r$ can also be written as $arrow(r)(t) = angle.l sin(2t), cos(2t) angle.r$.

## Arc length

> The **arc length** between $t = a$ and $t = b$ of a space curve with vector function $arrow(r)(t)$ is:
> $$ s = integral_a^b |arrow(r)'(t)| d t $$

$$ s = integral_a^b sqrt((x'(t))^2 + (y'(t))^2 + (z'(t))^2) d t $$

## Curvature

> The **curvature** of a space curve with vector function $arrow(r)(t)$ is:
> $$ kappa(t) = |(d arrow(T)) / (d s)| $$

But in practice, we will use
$$ kappa(t) = (|arrow(T)'(t)|) / (|arrow(r)'(t)|) = (|arrow(r)'(t) times arrow(r)''(t)|) / (|arrow(r)'(t)|^3) $$

Note: If only looking for a specific point, you can plug in $t$ early to avoid complex cross products.
