## 2025-12-03

For a point-like particle,

$$ arrow(L)_A = arrow(r)_A times arrow(p) $$

Remember that also,

$$ arrow(L) = I omega $$

To find the angular momentum of a system about a point $Q$ that is *not* the centre of mass:

$$ arrow(L)_Q = arrow(L)_(A Q) + arrow(L)_(B Q) + ... = arrow(r)_("CM" Q) times arrow(p) + I_"CM" arrow(omega) $$

Note that

$$ arrow(tau)_A = (d arrow(L)_A) / (d t) $$
