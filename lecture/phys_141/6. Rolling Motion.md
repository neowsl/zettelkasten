> **Rolling without slipping**
> A special case of combined rotational and translational motion.

In a time interval $Delta t$,
- $omega = (2 pi) / (Delta t)$
- $v_"CM" = (2 pi R) / (Delta t) = R omega$

When a dot is in contact with the floor,

$$ v_("dot" E) = v_("dot" "CM") + v_("CM" E) = -R omega hat(i) + R omega hat(i) = 0 $$
