Music notation is a graph of frequencies.

Doppler effect:
$$ f_o = (v plus.minus v_o) / (v minus.plus v_s) f_s $$
- $f_o$: Observed frequency
- $v$: Velocity of wave
- $v_o$: Velocity of observer
- $v_s$: Velocity of source
- $f_s$: Source frequency

## 2026-04-17

1. Waves have energy

$$ I = P / A $$
- $P$: Power
- $A$: Area (perpendicular to direction of wave)

Intensity of a wave is proportional to the square of its amplitude:
$$ I prop A^2 $$

What if amplitude is complex?
$$ A^2 = |A|^2 = A A^* $$
Where $A^*$ is the **complex conjugate** of $A$ (every $i$ is replaced with a $-i$).

---

For a spherical wave:
$$ I(r) prop (P_"source") / (4 pi r^2) $$
