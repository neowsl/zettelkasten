## 2026-03-30

We can think of variables in equations as the *adjectives* in a sentence.

$T$: Period (s)
$A$: Amplitude (m)
$phi$: Initial position

Observations:
- Motion repeats
- Period does not depend on amplitude.
- More mass = longer period
- Stiffer spring = shorter period

## 2026-04-06

Real form: $A cos(i omega t)$
Complex form: $A e^(i omega t)$

Phase change is not part of the exponent; instead, it is part of the amplitude, which can be complex.
