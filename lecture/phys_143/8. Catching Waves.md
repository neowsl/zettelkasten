For trapped waves:
- Only waves that fit are allowed.
- Each trapped wave has a specific energy.
- Energy is **quantised** (hence the term "quantum mechanics")!
- Allowed energies depend on the trap.
- There is a minimum energy.

Electrons move like waves, and are trapped by the nucleus.

### Making light

We don't see the electron waves, or what energy they have.

When an skips from one harmonic to another, it makes a photon of light with the photon's energy being the *difference* in energy between the two electron harmonics. The electron gave the energy it lost to the newly created photon.

The magnitude of these skips is what determines the energy (i.e. colour) of the photon!

Fun fact: The water in your eyes restrict the range of colour you can see!
