## 2026-03-06

The **Dirac delta function** represents an "instantaneous" force (or **impulse**) on a system.

$$
delta_c (t) = cases(
  0 quad & forall t != c,
  infinity quad & t = c
)
$$
$$ "such that" integral_(-infinity)^infinity delta_c (t) = 1 $$

$$ laplace(delta(t - a)) = e^(-a s) $$
