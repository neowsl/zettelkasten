## 2026-05-11: Regular Expressions

Subsets of strings are called **languages**.

**Regular expressions** over $Sigma$:
- Basis:
	- $epsilon$ is a regular expression (could also include $emptyset$).
	- $a$ is a regular expression for any $a in Sigma$.
- Recursive step:
	- If $A$ and $B$ are regular expressions, then so are:
		- $A union B$ (matches all strings that match either $A$ or $B$ or both)
		- $A B$ (matches all strings where the first part is $A$ and the second part is $B$)
		- $A^*$ (matches all strings that have any number of adjacent $A$s)
