> Intellect is the ability to hit a target others can't hit. Intuition is the ability to hit a target others can't see.

In the age of AI, intuition is difficult to develop. Intuition is trained from deep cognition over a problem, which LLMs are able to "shortcut" now. I like to think of solving problems like going to the gym. AI can lift heavy weights easily, but you don't go to the gym for the action of moving weights; you go to train your own muscles. In a similar way, I shouldn't reach for AI to solve *every* immediate problem - that struggle and [[failure]] is necessary for my own development as well.

At some point, AI will not be able to solve a difficult problem. That's when it's up to the programmer needs to be able to *reveal* the limits of AI, which is where intuition is the key [[value function]] to provide *foresight* into the correct path to take.

The way you train intuition is by pursuing problems that you may not necessarily have a correct answer. One approach is to enforce **constraints** on existing solutions to force consideration of [[Design decision|design decisions]].
