See also: [The Sudarium of Oviedo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudarium_of_Oviedo)

First-century dated shroud with image of man visible at > 6 feet away. Signs of Jesus:
- Blood from thorn wounds located near temple
- Blood from nails driven through wrists (not hands)
- Limestone traces on feet, knees, and nose (from falling down while carrying cross)
- Same key features as the Sudarium
- 34 trillion watts of energy required to leave the imprint
