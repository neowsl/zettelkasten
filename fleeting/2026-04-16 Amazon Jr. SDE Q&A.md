Top 3 Leadership Principles for Jrs.:
1. Learn and Be Curious
2. Bias for Action
3. Ownership

**What to do in college to prepare for a job in tech?**
- Research seminars for *technical aptitude*, finding something to dive deep into
	- Pro Sean tip: Reach out to professors for specific projects you want
- English classes for understanding technical docs

**Ownership?**
- Can propose project ideas?

**Introductions**
- Name
- School, major
- Journey: why interested in tech / walk through the journey / set up a "pipeline"
- Feel out with each interviewer, don't ramble if unnecessary
- What was your role and what were your contributions?
- Not really judged, feel free to talk

**AI?**
- Be adaptable
- Doesn't cover fundamentals
- Use AI to learn

**Advice**
- Be specific in your studies
- You are there to learn
- Understand company culture, LPs for Amazon
