From [[Ilya Sutskever – We're moving from the age of scaling to the age of research]]:

There's a Silicon Valley saying, "Ideas are cheap, execution is everything". However, Ilya posits that as we scale, we will realise that we need more ideas. There is a balance between [[scaling]] and [[research]].
